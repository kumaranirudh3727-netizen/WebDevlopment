Yeh kahani O. Henry ne likhi thi. Is kahani mein do young artists, Sue aur Johnsy, New York mein saath rehti hain. Sardi ke mausam mein Johnsy bahut bimar pad jaati hai aur use pneumonia ho jaata hai. Woh itni nirash ho jaati hai ki apni khidki se bahar ek bel ko dekhti rehti hai aur sochti hai ki jab is bel ka aakhri patta gir jaega, tab woh bhi mar jaegi. Dhire dhire saare patte girte jaate hain aur sirf ek patta bachi rehta hai. Raat bhar tez aandhi aur baarish hoti hai, phir bhi woh patta nahi girta. Yeh dekhkar Johnsy ki ummeed wapas aa jaati hai aur woh theek hone lagti hai. Baad mein pata chalta hai ki unka pados mein rehne wala budha painter Behrman, jo khud ek asafal kalakar tha, usne us aakhri patte ko raat bhar baarish aur thand mein baahar jakar deewaar par paint kiya tha — taaki Johnsy ki ummeed na toote. Us raat ki thand se Behrman khud bimar pad gaya aur kuch dino mein uski maut ho gayi. Uska woh aakhri patta hi uski zindagi ki sabse badi
